# Approach A — initialization-robust cross-circuit refinement

**Recommended main project. Status: proposed extension; no real-circuit results.**

## Motivation and novelty boundary

MaskRegulate already refines existing layouts and reports held-out chip performance. The course contribution must go further than rerunning that test. We propose a controlled study of **joint shift in circuit family and initializer**, with a small training extension that can be isolated experimentally. [Baseline paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/fe224a60b878e79d5b3d79d7f113f76b-Paper-Conference.pdf)

Normalization and training on diverse states are established machine-learning techniques. Claim the specific formulation, tested combination and empirical conclusions; do not claim these ingredients as newly invented.

## Method

Keep the baseline policy, selected-macro schedule, grid interface and exact objective initially. Upstream already uses a fixed grid and train-calibrated reward extrema; the baseline is not unnormalized. Audit those quantities before proposing per-episode or physically referenced scales. Candidate features include sizes `(w/W,h/H)`, areas `A/(WH)` and comparable wire-cost-map scales only where the current representation lacks them. Define zero-scale fallbacks. Do not normalize away macro aspect ratios or pin-offset information that affects legality and wiring.

Train on multiple legal initial layouts per training circuit, generated by recorded seeds and different levels of legal displacement. When a second true initializer is available, add it to the mixture. Maintain a strict distinction between perturbations of one initializer and a different optimization algorithm.

The policy adjusts macros one at a time. The original OLD/NEW occupancy mask permits overlap with unadjusted macros during a pass. Reproduce that exact passwise interface, validate complete-pass outputs, and retain the last checked feasible layout on time interruption. A no-op must satisfy the current mask; it is not automatically feasible. An always-legal relocation variant is a separate experiment. Give every optimizer the same ability to retain the best feasible endpoint.

## Identifying experiment

| Variant | Normalization | Start distribution |
|---|---|---|
| Original | Upstream | Single |
| N | Proposed transfer-reference scaling | Single |
| M | Upstream | Mixed |
| N+M | Proposed transfer-reference scaling | Mixed |

Train with comparable environment-step budgets and record total GPU/CPU hours. More mixed-start data is additional compute; quantify it instead of treating it as free. Evaluate all four on a circuit-disjoint validation set before choosing any normalization constants or mixture weights. Final tests use frozen parameters and a held-out initializer where feasible.

Include identity, random legal moves, greedy search and tuned simulated annealing. Pair starts and seeds. Compare both equal wall time and equal objective evaluations, since one can favor different implementations. Log preprocessing, legalizer and export time separately and in totals.

## What would support the hypothesis?

Greater per-circuit improvement than original RL under joint shift, without more violations, and a quality-versus-time advantage over the strongest search control. Gains on only training circuits indicate overfitting. Gains only from a weak starting layout may be useful but do not establish robust improvement of already optimized layouts.

## Failure modes and useful negative results

- Normalization changes little because upstream wire masks already remove much scale dependence.
- Mixed starts merely spend more training compute.
- Search reaches the same outcome with less total development and deployment cost.
- Dense rewards encourage greedy short-term improvements that miss coordinated macro moves.
- Initializer shift destroys learned spatial preferences even when graph structure is familiar.

A documented phase diagram of these effects is a meaningful systematic-evaluation contribution if the protocol is controlled and conclusions are scoped to the available circuit families.

Implement after the upstream environment and objective round-trip on one real circuit. Start with training/evaluation plumbing, seeding before constructors, and validation-only checkpoint selection.
